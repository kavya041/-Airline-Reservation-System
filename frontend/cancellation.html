Refund Policy & Eligibility

  • Full Refund: If cancelled more than 48 hours before departure (minus ₹200 fee).
  • Partial Refund: 24-48 hours before departure (₹500 fee applies).
  • Last Minute: 2-24 hours before departure (₹1000 fee applies).
  • Non-Refundable: Less than 2 hours before departure or after flight.
  • Procedure: Refund is initiated automatically upon confirmation. You will receive a confirmation email and SMS once processed.
